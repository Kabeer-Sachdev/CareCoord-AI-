# ST. JUDE METROPOLITAN HOSPITAL
## POLICY DOCUMENT: PATIENT DISCHARGE AND TRANSITION OF CARE PROCEDURE
Policy Reference: POL-DIS-2024-V2
Department: Clinical Quality, Nursing & Case Management
Effective Date: March 15, 2024
Review Cycle: Annual

1. PURPOSE
The goal of this policy is to facilitate safe, coordinated, and timely patient discharges, reduce unplanned 30-day hospital readmissions, and ensure continuity of outpatient medical care and medication management.

2. DISCHARGE TIMELINE AND ADVANCE PLANNING
2.1 Discharge planning begins within 24 hours of inpatient admission through multidisciplinary evaluation by Case Management and Social Services.
2.2 Target Daily Discharge Time: Patients scheduled for discharge should vacate inpatient beds by 11:00 AM on their discharge day. This standard turnaround ensures morning bed availability for ED and surgical admissions.
2.3 The attending physician must communicate the expected discharge date (EDD) to the patient, primary caregiver, and nursing staff at least 24 hours prior to anticipated departure.

3. CLINICAL CLEARANCE AND DISCHARGE ORDERS
3.1 A formal electronic Discharge Order must be signed by the attending physician or credentialed resident by 09:00 AM on the day of discharge.
3.2 Nursing discharge assessment verifies stable vital signs, independent ambulation or safe transfer capability, and resolution of acute clinical issues.
3.3 Discharge Medication Reconciliation: Clinical Pharmacist conducts comprehensive medication reconciliation, reviewing home medications against newly prescribed inpatient medications to prevent dangerous interactions or duplicate therapies.

4. PATIENT EDUCATION AND WRITTEN INSTRUCTIONS
4.1 The bedside nurse reviews the Written After-Visit Summary (AVS) with the patient and primary caregiver using the "Teach-Back" method.
4.2 Instructions must include:
    - Clear diagnosis summary in plain, non-technical language.
    - Prescribed medications, dosages, frequency, and instructions on side effects.
    - Specific dietary restrictions, wound care instructions, and activity limitations.
    - Scheduled follow-up appointments with outpatient specialists within 7 to 14 days.
    - "Red Flag" warning symptoms requiring immediate return to the Emergency Department (e.g., chest pain, shortness of breath, sudden fever >101°F).

5. DISCHARGE TRANSIT LOUNGE AND TRANSPORTATION
5.1 Patients cleared clinically who are awaiting family pickup or pharmacy prescription fulfillment after 11:00 AM are transitioned to the comfortable Discharge Hospitality Lounge.
5.2 Wheelchair escorts provided by patient transport staff are mandatory from the hospital unit to the patient's departure vehicle.
