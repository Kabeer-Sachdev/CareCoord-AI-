# ST. JUDE METROPOLITAN HOSPITAL
## POLICY DOCUMENT: HOSPITAL VISITING HOURS AND VISITOR CODE OF CONDUCT
Policy Reference: POL-VIS-2024-V1
Department: Hospital Administration, Security & Patient Advocacy
Effective Date: April 1, 2024
Review Cycle: Annual

1. PURPOSE AND PATIENT RIGHTS
St. Jude Metropolitan Hospital recognizes that the support of family and loved ones promotes patient healing, emotional reassurance, and positive clinical recovery. This policy balances patient visitation rights with the necessity of infection control, clinical safety, and restorative rest.

2. STANDARD VISITING HOURS BY DEPARTMENT
2.1 General Medical and Surgical Inpatient Wards:
    - Daily Visiting Hours: 10:00 AM to 08:00 PM.
    - Quiet Hours (Mandatory rest period): 08:00 PM to 07:00 AM.
2.2 Intensive Care Units (ICU, CCU, NICU):
    - Visiting Hours: 11:00 AM to 02:00 PM and 05:00 PM to 08:00 PM daily.
    - Quiet Rest Break (No visitors permitted except by clinical exception): 02:00 PM to 05:00 PM.
2.3 Maternity / Labor & Delivery:
    - Primary support person: 24/7 unrestricted access with hospital partner badge.
    - General visitors: 11:00 AM to 08:00 PM.
2.4 Pediatric Inpatient Ward:
    - Parents or legal guardians: 24/7 unrestricted bedside presence. One parent may stay overnight in sleeper recliner.
    - Sibling visitation: 02:00 PM to 07:00 PM, subject to symptom screening.

3. VISITOR LIMITS AND IDENTIFICATION
3.1 A maximum of two (2) visitors are permitted at the bedside simultaneously in General Wards.
3.2 A maximum of two (2) immediate family members are permitted in ICU rooms at any one time.
3.3 All visitors must sign in at the Security Lobby Desk, present photo identification, and obtain a dated Visitor Security Badge which must remain visibly worn above the waist at all times.
3.4 Children under the age of 12 must be supervised by an adult visitor at all times and are not permitted in high-risk units (e.g., Burn Unit, Bone Marrow Transplant) without prior attending approval.

4. HEALTH SCREENING AND INFECTION PREVENTION
4.1 Any individual experiencing fever, productive cough, respiratory symptoms, vomiting, or diarrhea within the past 48 hours is strictly prohibited from entering patient care units.
4.2 Hand hygiene with hospital-provided alcohol hand sanitizer is mandatory upon entering and exiting every patient room.
4.3 Visitors entering rooms designated under Contact, Droplet, or Airborne isolation must wear the required Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) as directed by nursing signage.
