# ST. JUDE METROPOLITAN HOSPITAL
## POLICY DOCUMENT: INPATIENT BED ALLOCATION AND UTILIZATION GUIDELINES
Policy Reference: POL-BED-2024-V5
Department: Bed Management, Nursing Administration & Patient Flow
Effective Date: January 15, 2024
Review Cycle: Annual

1. OVERVIEW AND OBJECTIVES
Efficient bed management is essential to deliver high-quality acute care, eliminate emergency department crowding, prevent elective surgery cancellations, and minimize hospital-acquired infections.

2. BED ALLOCATION HIERARCHY AND CLINICAL PRIORITIZATION
When inpatient bed capacity exceeds 85% occupancy, bed assignments follow this strict priority sequence:
- Priority 1 (Highest): Emergent Level 1/2 transfers from Emergency Department and Code Blue resuscitations.
- Priority 2: Post-operative surgical recovery patients requiring ICU or Step-down monitoring following urgent/emergency surgery.
- Priority 3: Scheduled elective surgical patients requiring pre-booked post-operative beds.
- Priority 4: Inpatient inter-ward transfers due to step-up clinical deterioration.
- Priority 5: Elective non-surgical admissions from outpatient clinics or physician referrals.

3. INFECTION CONTROL AND ISOLATION PLACEMENT
3.1 Airborne Isolation (Negative Pressure Rooms): Mandatory for confirmed or suspected pulmonary Tuberculosis, Measles, Varicella, and Disseminated Zoster.
3.2 Contact Isolation: Patients colonized or infected with Methicillin-Resistant Staphylococcus Aureus (MRSA), Vancomycin-Resistant Enterococcus (VRE), or Clostridioides difficile must be placed in single-occupancy rooms or cohorted with patients possessing identical microbiological profiles.
3.3 Droplet Isolation: Required for influenza, meningococcal disease, and respiratory syncytial virus (RSV).

4. BED CLEANING AND TURNAROUND STANDARDS
4.1 Following patient discharge, Environmental Services (EVS) receives an automated notification.
4.2 Standard Ward Bed Turnaround Target: 45 minutes from discharge confirmation to "Clean & Ready" status.
4.3 Isolation / Decontamination Bed Turnaround Target: 75 minutes, including ultraviolet-C (UV-C) terminal disinfection.
4.4 Overcrowding Escalation ("Surge Level 1-3"): If more than 5 patients are waiting in ED for >2 hours without bed placement, the Bed Management Committee initiates surge measures, including opening auxiliary observation units.
